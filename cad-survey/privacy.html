Privacy Policy

CAD Survey app for Android · By Eng. Ali

Effective date: 11 November 2026

In short: CAD Survey works on your phone or tablet. Your projects, drawings, points, photos and voice notes stay on your device. We do not run analytics, show ads or ask you to create an account. The only information we receive is what you choose to send us yourself.

1. Who we are

CAD Survey ("the app") is a field app for land surveyors and civil engineers, developed by Eng. Ali Abdulkhaleq Dakhil Hilfi (CAD & SURVEY), Basra, Iraq. Contact: eng.alicadsurvey@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your device

  • Projects, drawings (DXF, PDF, KML), points, measurements, reports and exports.
  • Site notes: photos, voice notes and text you add.
  • Settings, your profile details (name, company, logo) and your activation status.

The app does not upload any of this. If you uninstall the app, Android deletes the app's data unless you saved a backup or exported files yourself.

3. Permissions and why we ask

  • Location (only when you tap GPS): shows your position as a dot on the drawing. It is used on the device and is not sent to us.
  • Camera: take site photos and read point tables from a photo.
  • Microphone (only when you hold the mic button): short voice notes saved on the device.
  • Notifications: a reminder on the due date of a site note or task.
  • Files you open or share to the app: the app reads only the file you choose. The original file is never changed.
  • Internet: used only for the items in sections 4 and 5.

4. Reading text from photos (Google ML Kit)

"Read from photo" recognises text on the device with Google ML Kit. Your photo and its text are not sent anywhere. Like other apps that use ML Kit, the ML Kit library may send Google limited diagnostic data (device model, Android version, app version, a per-installation identifier and performance figures) to keep the feature working. This data is encrypted in transit and is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Things that happen only when you tap them

  • Share (WhatsApp, e-mail, Drive and others): the file or coordinates you choose go to the app you pick.
  • Google Maps link: sends the selected point's coordinates to Google Maps.
  • Videos and social links (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram): open in those apps or your browser.
  • Report a problem: prepares a message (with a screenshot and app/device details) that you decide whether to send.

6. Activation

Pro features are unlocked with an activation code. The app shows a short Device ID that you may send to us yourself to receive a code linked to your device. The code is checked on the device. The app does not send your Device ID or code to any server.

7. Information we receive from you

When you contact us for a trial, a subscription or support, we receive only what you send: for example your name, e-mail or phone number, your Device ID, and your message or files. We use it only to issue your code and help you. We do not sell or share it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

8. What the app does not do

  • No analytics or tracking by us, and no advertising.
  • No upload of your drawings, photos, voice notes or project data.
  • No user accounts or sign-in.
  • No selling of personal data.

9. Children

The app is a professional tool for adults (18 years and over) and is not directed to children.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. For any privacy question or a deletion request, write to eng.alicadsurvey@gmail.com.
